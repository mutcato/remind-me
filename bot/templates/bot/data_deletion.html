Data Deletion

The only data the bot holds about you comes from comments in which you @mentioned it.

1. Delete the comment

Delete your comment on Instagram: the reminder can no longer be delivered and the record is removed at the next cleanup.

2. Request deletion

Send a direct message to @remindme.in on Instagram saying "delete my data", or email {{ contact_email }} with your Instagram username. We match the request to your account and delete every record within 30 days, then confirm.

What is deleted

The reminder record, the comment text and id, the username, the post permalink and any generated reply text. Deletion is irreversible and cancels pending reminders.
